Set up automatic estimates

Automatic estimates require iOS 26.4 or later and Apple's expanded Screen Time access.

  1. Open Automatic setup and review what the app uses.
  2. Tap Enable automatic estimates and allow access when Apple asks. If access is already available, tap Start automatic estimates.
  3. In DFE, open Settings and check that Screen Time shows Connected.

DFE uses the app activity Apple makes available, without daily forms or individual app selection. If the app loses access, open the same section in Settings and choose Reconnect Screen Time when it appears.

Why has no daily result appeared yet?

Your first estimate can appear after your setup day ends and Apple provides usable app activity. iOS controls when background updates run. If a day is still pending, check your Screen Time connection in DFE and try opening the app again later.

If expanded access is unavailable, DFE keeps new days pending and shows any earlier saved estimates. Apple's published requirements for App Store users include both an iPhone located in the EU and an Apple Account set to an EU country or region. Our Swiss tests used development access. Read Apple's Screen Time access requirements.

Understanding your daily estimate

In Today, check the completed date beside the estimate. For a comparison, open Apple's daily Screen Time report for the same date and iPhone. The app activity Apple shares with DFE can differ from the total in that report. A pending day has no usable estimate yet.

DFE groups available app time into categories on your device. Apps it cannot categorise appear under Other apps. Browser app time can count, while website records are excluded. The model uses assumptions for data traffic, Wi-Fi share, screen brightness, GPS and Bluetooth, so the result is an estimate of carbon emissions. See How the estimate works in DFE Settings.

Your first saved estimate gives the garden a starting point. Later, the garden compares each estimate with the average of up to seven earlier saved days. A greener garden means a lower estimate relative to those days.

App and Home Screen appearance

In DFE Settings, choose Appearance to switch between Light, Dark and System. Light is the default. To change the app icon's appearance, touch and hold the iPhone Home Screen, then choose Edit and Customize. The icon follows your iPhone's Home Screen setting.

Adding the garden widget

  1. Touch and hold an empty area of your iPhone Home Screen, then tap Edit and Add Widget.
  2. Find Digital Footprint and choose Your garden.
  3. Choose a small or medium size, then tap Add Widget. Tap the widget any time to open Today.

The widget shows your latest saved garden and its date. If Apple allows access, it can calculate the previous completed day when iOS gives it time to run, even while DFE is closed. DFE adds that result to History the next time it runs. The widget may keep showing an earlier date while a new day is pending.

If DFE Settings shows Resume widget updates under Screen Time, tap it to review access. Removing the widget leaves your saved history available in DFE. See Apple's widget guide.

iPhone Duo and Apple Watch

On the wider iPhone Duo display, Today puts the saved estimate beside the garden when there is room.

The optional Watch app requires watchOS 10 or later and a paired iPhone. On your iPhone, open DFE Settings and turn on Show my garden on Apple Watch. The Watch receives a summary of your latest saved phone estimate. App names and usage times stay on your iPhone.

If the Watch shows an older date, tap Refresh in the Watch app. If it cannot reach the phone, open DFE on your iPhone. Turning off Watch sharing, disconnecting Screen Time or deleting entries clears the summary when the Watch next receives the update.

Optional garden updates

In DFE Settings, open A morning moment and turn on Daily garden update. Allow notifications when Apple asks. This setting is off by default.

DFE schedules one local reminder per day at about 08:00 for the next 30 mornings and renews the schedule when it can run. iOS can show reminders even after you close DFE from the app switcher. Focus, Scheduled Summary and your notification settings can change when they appear.

A reminder can arrive while yesterday's estimate is still pending. If a fresh result is saved in time, the reminder may describe your garden for that day. Tapping either kind of reminder opens Today.

Turn Daily garden update off to cancel pending reminders. This setting is separate from Screen Time. If reminders are missing, also check DFE's notification settings on your iPhone.

Your choices and local data

To stop new Screen Time imports, open Connection details in DFE Settings and choose Disconnect Screen Time. You can still read your saved estimates.

To clear DFE's saved data, choose Delete all entries and confirm. This clears your history and the widget summary, resets calculation and appearance settings, and turns off Watch sharing and daily reminders. Messages already shown in Notification Center stay there until you clear them.

If automatic estimates were connected and still have permission, they stay connected with the standard settings. DFE and the widget skip days before the reset. A paused widget stays paused. Deleting entries leaves Apple's Screen Time records and existing backups intact. Read how DFE handles data deletion.

Contact the project

For privacy or support questions, contact the DFE Team at digital-footprint-explorer.init@zhaw.ch.

Include your app version, iOS version and the steps that led to the problem. For an import or widget issue, open Connection details, then Automatic update checks. Use Copy update checks to share a short technical summary if you wish. It excludes app names, usage amounts and estimate values. Keep Screen Time history, passwords and device identifiers out of your message.
